📖 What is this?

Soulful Notes is a free, aesthetic personal journaling web app designed for people who want to reflect on their days, track their moods, build good habits, and plan their life — all in one beautiful place.

No ads. No subscriptions. Just you and your thoughts.

✨ Features

📖
Journal

Write daily entries with mood tracking. Your thoughts, saved forever.

✅
To-Do List

Manage tasks by priority. Stay on top of what matters.

🌱
Life Planning

Set goals across life areas and track your progress visually.

🔥
Habit Tracker

Build daily habits and maintain your streak week by week.

🔒 Your data is private

All your data is stored securely in the cloud and tied to your account. Only you can see it. We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

💌 How to get started

  1. Create a free account with your email
  2. Confirm your email address
  3. Start writing your first journal entry
  4. Add habits you want to build
  5. Set your life goals and track progress
